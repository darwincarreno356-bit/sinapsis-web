LA HORA EXACTA

Entertainment program based on footage material. Primetime. Co Hosted by Teté Coustarot and Boy Olmi.

EXPERIMENTORES 1 (2017)

Science program for the whole family (26×25).
Hosted by Nazareno Casero.
Adaptation, development and production of the original peruvian format. Winner of the Premio Fan Chile for Best Children’s series (Non Fiction).
Filmed in Lima (Peru) and Buenos Aires (Argentina).
Paka paka Argentina / TVP.

EXPERIMENTORES 2 (2021)

Science program for the whole family (26×25).

COMBATE

Competition reality (physical challenges, talent and trivia).
Hosted by Guillermo Catalano, Thaigo Batistuta, Larua Fernández and “Pollo” Álvarez.
A success in its daily and weekly as well as digital versions.
Production, live broadcasting and general direction. 2014/2016 – El Nueve. Argentina.

#MARCHA

Awarded transmedia documentary project (TV series, feature film and webisodes).
The Nazi genocide from the adolescent gaze. Recorded in Poland, Israel, Argentina and the Vatican City.
With the participation of S.S. Pope Francis I.
TVP Argentina / Cont.ar / Cine.ar

RUMUVI

Web series (13×13).
Dramedy. Script, production and general direction.
Filmed in the province of Corrientes (Argentina).

Awarded internationally for Best Direction in the BAWebFest, Rome Web Awards and a Silver Medal in the Excellence Awards, Best editing, Best Leading Actor and Best Trailer in the Rome Web Awards, Best Supporting Actress and Best Musical Band in the BAWebFest. Nominated for Best Screenplay and Best Drama in the Seoul Web Fest as well as Best Actress in the Río WebFest.
Best Drama Indie Series Awards and Best Dramedy in the NYC Webfest. Official Selection in the Hollyweb Festival.
