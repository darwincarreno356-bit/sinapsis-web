## Who we are

**Sinapsis Producción **is an audiovisual production company specialized in creating, developing, formatting and producing content. It is led by renowned professionals with more than thirty years of experience working in Argentina, United States, Mexico, Chile, Peru, Colombia, Ecuador, Uruguay, Bolivia and Spain as well as on pan-regional screens.

We produce and develop for: Netflix – NBC Telemundo – Canal 9 de Argentina – Paka Paka – Encuentro – TV Pública – América TV.

We have offices in Buenos Aires, Argentina and Miami, FL. USA.
